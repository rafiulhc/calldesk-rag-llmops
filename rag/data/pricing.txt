Calldesk Pricing and Plans
(Source: calldesk.app/pricing, summarized October 2026)

Starter plan: 599 USD per month. Includes 600 minutes per month (about 300 or more calls), 1 phone number, job booking by email link and ICS calendar invite, answers from your FAQs, an email summary of every call, instant SMS confirmation to callers, and email support.

Growth plan: 899 USD per month, marked as the most popular plan. Includes 1,200 minutes per month (about 600 or more calls) and 2 phone numbers. Adds on-call escalation, voicemail transcription, bilingual English and Spanish with automatic language detection, the job log with New, Scheduled and Done statuses, and priority support.

Scale plan: 1,299 USD per month. Includes 2,400 minutes per month (about 1,200 or more calls) and 3 phone numbers. Adds a custom voice or model through ElevenLabs, dedicated 24/7 support, and team permissions with an audit trail.

How call estimates are calculated: The call counts assume an average call length of about 2 minutes. Real call volume varies by business.

What counts as minutes: Minutes include on-call conversation time and the AI processing the call needs. Transferred calls and voicemail also count toward minutes.

Extra minutes: Every plan can buy overage top-ups of 300 minutes for 100 USD. Top-ups are purchased in Admin, under Billing.

Annual billing: Paying annually gives 20 percent off and 20 percent more minutes.

Changing plans: Customers can change plans at any time from the billing portal. The change takes effect at the next renewal.

Free trial: Every plan has a 7-day free trial, and it can be cancelled at any time. The trial number is provisioned automatically and starts answering calls right away.

Larger needs: Businesses that need more than 5,000 minutes per month or a white-label version should contact sales at support@calldesk.app.

Billing: Checkout and billing are handled by Stripe, and receipts are sent to the email address given at checkout. Stripe also handles tax collection.
